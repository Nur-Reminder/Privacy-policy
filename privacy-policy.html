No Data Collection

Nur Reminder does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. We have no servers, no user accounts, and no analytics. Your information never leaves your device.

📍

Location Data

Your GPS coordinates are used exclusively on-device to calculate accurate prayer times for your area. Location data is temporarily stored in your device's local storage only to avoid re-requesting permissions on every launch. It is never sent to any external server except the Aladhan.com public API to fetch prayer times — using only latitude/longitude, no identity information.

🔔

Notifications

Push notification preferences are stored entirely on your device. Notification tokens are handled locally by expo-notifications and are never transmitted to our servers because we have none.

🎵

Audio Preferences

Your selected Muezzin voice preference is saved in your device's local storage only. Adhan audio files are streamed from public third-party CDNs (Islamcan.com) when you play them. No usage data is collected from these streams.

☁

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services for app functionality: • Aladhan.com API — Fetches prayer time data using your coordinates. See their privacy policy at aladhan.com. • Islamcan.com — Streams adhan audio files. No personal data is sent. None of these services receive any personally identifiable information from this app.

👤

Children's Privacy

Nur Reminder is suitable for all ages. Since we collect no personal data whatsoever, there are no concerns related to children's privacy under COPPA or similar regulations.

🔒

Data Security

All preferences and cached prayer times are stored using standard iOS/Android secure local storage (AsyncStorage). Since no data is transmitted to our servers, there is no risk of a server-side data breach affecting you.

🔄

Policy Updates

If this Privacy Policy ever changes, the updated version will be included in the next app update. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

🗑 Data Deletion

Since Nur Reminder stores all data exclusively on your device, you have complete control over deletion at any time.

To delete all app data:

1
Go to your device Settings
2
Find "Nur Reminder" in your app list
3
Tap "Offload App" or "Delete App"
4
All local data is permanently removed
There is no "account deletion" request needed because we never created an account for you. Uninstalling the app removes 100% of your data instantly.
